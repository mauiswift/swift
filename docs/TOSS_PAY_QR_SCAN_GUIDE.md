# TOSS Pay — QR Scan Guide (SwiftPay QRPH)

This guide explains how to scan and pay a SwiftPay QR code using **TOSS Pay**.

## Important notes

- I can’t generate **real** screenshots from the TOSS app here. If you provide actual screenshots, we can embed them and make this guide “1:1”.
- SwiftPay **QRPH** is **PHP-oriented**. For KRW/USDT collection, use the KRW channels / USDT top-up flows in SwiftPay.

## In-dashboard version (recommended)

Open the dashboard page:

- `/help/toss-pay-qr`

You can also prefill the QR payload:

- `/help/toss-pay-qr?qr=<QRPH_PAYLOAD>&amount=<AMOUNT>`

Paste your SwiftPay QRPH/EMV payload (usually starts with `000201`) to preview the QR.

## Steps (TOSS app)

1. Open **TOSS**
2. Go to **Pay / Payments**
3. Tap **Scan QR**
4. Scan the **SwiftPay QRPH** displayed on your SwiftPay checkout (or on `/help/toss-pay-qr`)
5. Confirm **merchant name** and **amount**
6. Complete the payment
7. Save the receipt / screenshot the success page (if needed for verification)
